# Week 12 – Final Analytics, Dashboard & Delivery

## Overview

Week 12 is the final delivery phase of the CadetX Warehouse Analytics project. It consolidates the final KPIs, business insights, recommendations, dashboard and presentation into a complete analytical output.

## Final Deliverables

### 1. Final KPI Summary
Location:
`analysis/kpi/final_kpi_summary.csv`

The final KPI summary contains:
- Total Products
- Total Inventory Records
- Total Sales Order Lines
- Total Suppliers
- Total Branches
- Total Customers
- Total Units Sold
- Total Sales Value
- Total Current Stock

### 2. Final Business Insights
Location:
`analysis/insights/final_insights.csv`

The insights summarize the major findings from the final KPI results, including sales volume, inventory position, supplier network, branch coverage and customer base.

Location:
`recommendations/final_recommendations.csv`

The recommendations focus on:
- Customer-level sales analysis
- Sales trend monitoring
- Revenue monitoring
- Inventory monitoring
- Demand and replenishment planning
- Reducing excess stock and potential stockout risk

`dashboard/final/`

### 5. Final Presentation

`presentation/`

The presentation summarizes the project overview, final KPIs, business insights, recommendations, final dashboard and conclusion.
